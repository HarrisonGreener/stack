Questions

Does the Stack Puck record my music?

Yes, and that's the point. It records your own copy in high resolution as you play it, so you can listen to your exact pressing anywhere. You can switch to the licensed studio version at any time.

Do I need to play the whole record?

To record your copy, yes, once, in real time: play it once, keep it forever. The studio version is available as soon as you scan the barcode, so you can listen straight away.

How do you know I own it?

The barcode identifies the release, and recording it proves you have the physical copy, because a record has to be played in full on your turntable. Partner releases will also include a one-time Stack tag inside the sleeve.

What happens if I sell or lose a record?

Remove it from your Stack and it stops streaming. Records are tied to the owner who scanned them, so a copy can only be in one Stack at a time.

Does it change how my music sounds?

No. The Stack Puck passes your audio straight through to your speakers untouched. It only records alongside.

What do I need?

A turntable or CD player, your speakers or amp, the Stack app, and a Stack subscription. Everything's in the box: the Stack Puck, two cables and a USB-C charger.
